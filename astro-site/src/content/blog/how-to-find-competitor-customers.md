## Where the evidence comes from

We built 28 verified customer lists covering 2,999 companies. For the largest one, Vanta (353 companies), we tracked every signal type. Here is where the evidence came from:

| Source | Companies found |
|---|---|
| The customer's own website or security page | 126 |
| Partner, agency and auditor case studies | 68 |
| Tech-stack databases | 53 |
| Events, awards and speaker lists | 46 |
| Trust centers | 26 |
| Blogs and press | 24 |
| "We use X" posts by customers | 23 |
| Job posts | 22 |
| LinkedIn posts | 14 |
| Review sites | 12 |
| Video and webinars | 11 |
| Communities (Reddit, Hacker News) | 3 |

*A company can show up in more than one source.*

The lesson: the vendor's logo wall shows the brands it chose. The rest of the customers sit on other people's pages.

## The 12 places to look

1. **Case studies on the vendor's site.** Start here, but don't stop here.
2. **The customer's own trust or security page.** It often names the platform behind it.
3. **Partner and auditor case studies.** Agencies and auditors name their clients.
4. **Tech-stack databases.** Useful leads, weakest proof. Verify before you reach out.
5. **Events and awards.** Customer award winners and conference speakers.
6. **Blogs and press.** "X selects Y", "we switched to Y".
7. **Customer posts.** "We use Y" from a named employee.
8. **Job posts.** A role that requires the tool means the team runs it.
9. **LinkedIn posts.** Admins and leaders talk about their tools.
10. **Review sites.** G2, Capterra and TrustRadius reviews with a named company.
11. **Video.** Customer webinars and testimonials.
12. **Communities and public records.** Reddit, Hacker News, and for non-SaaS markets, government contracts and board approvals.

## How to grade what you find

- **A:** two or more independent sources agree.
- **B:** one direct source, like a case study, a job post or a named review.
- **C:** tech-stack database only. Treat it as a lead, not a fact.

## When the digital trail is thin

Manufacturers, distributors and service companies leave far fewer traces than software companies. Three things still work:

- **Public contracts.** Cities, counties, utilities and state agencies publish their vendors in contracts and board approvals.
- **Your CRM.** Find the patterns behind your best customers and look for more companies like them.
- **ABM.** For large accounts, reach the whole buying group with ads, LinkedIn, email and calls until the meeting is booked.
