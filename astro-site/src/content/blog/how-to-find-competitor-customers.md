## Why the vendor's logo wall isn't enough

A vendor shows maybe 5% of its customers, and only the ones it chose. The rest are hiding in plain sight on third-party pages.

## The 12 places to look

1. Case studies and customer stories on the vendor's site
2. The customer's own trust center or security page
3. Job posts that list the tool as a requirement
4. G2 reviews with a named company or industry
5. Capterra and Trustpilot reviews
6. LinkedIn posts from the people who run the tool
7. Tech-stack databases (weakest signal, check it)
8. Integration and partner pages
9. Agency and consultant case studies
10. Company blogs ("how we use X")
11. Press releases and trade press
12. Reddit and community threads

## How to rank what you find

- **A**: two or more independent sources agree.
- **B**: one direct source (case study, job post, named review).
- **C**: tech-stack database only. Verify before you reach out.
