## What Vanta customers have in common

Read enough of these case studies and the same story repeats.

**A deal forced it.** A big prospect sent a security questionnaire or asked for a SOC 2 report, and the company didn't have one. BreachRx and Charityvest say it almost word for word: they needed SOC 2 to close deals faster.

**They're lean.** BVNK talks about a compliance program "for a lean team". These companies don't have a security department yet. Someone in engineering or ops owns compliance on top of their real job.

**They sell to enterprises.** AI companies (Anthropic, AssemblyAI, Coxwave), fintech (BVNK), healthtech (Cedar) and sales tools (Clay, Ashby). All of them sell into buyers with procurement teams.

**They're moving up.** The trigger for Vanta is the first serious enterprise deal. That usually lands somewhere between seed and Series C.

## How to turn the list into meetings

A list doesn't book meetings. The angle does.

**1. Lead with what they already did.** "Saw you run your trust center on Vanta" is specific and true. Anyone can check it.

**2. Talk about what comes after the audit.** Getting SOC 2 is a project. Keeping it is a job nobody hired for. That's where your product fits.

**3. Time it.** The best moments to reach out:

- A new trust center went live
- They announced "we achieved SOC 2" on LinkedIn or their blog
- They posted a job for a first security or compliance hire
- 10 to 12 months after the announcement, when the first renewal and audit come up

**Example first email:**

> Subject: your trust center
>
> Hi Maya, saw your trust center runs on Vanta and you passed SOC 2 this spring.
>
> The audit is the easy part. Keeping every control green with no security team is where most teams fall behind.
>
> We help companies your size keep that running without a full-time hire. Worth a 15-minute look?

No trash talk about Vanta. You're not selling against the tool, you're selling into the gap it leaves.

## How to check a company yourself

1. Go to their website and look for a Trust, Security or Compliance link in the footer.
2. If the trust page URL is app.vanta.com/[company]/trust, they use Vanta.
3. Search their LinkedIn and blog for "SOC 2" and "Vanta".
4. Check open job posts. Security and IT roles name the tools they run.
5. Write down the source URL. A name without a source is a guess.

## Mistakes to skip

- **Trusting tech-stack databases alone.** They catch a script on a page, not a contract. That's why grade C rows are flagged.
- **Pitching the same week they passed the audit.** They're celebrating, not buying. Wait for the gap to show up.
- **Writing to the CEO.** At this stage the owner is the CTO, the head of engineering or whoever ran the audit. Their name is in the case study or the LinkedIn announcement.
