## What AuditBoard customers have in common

**They're public or regulated.** Banks, healthcare, consumer brands and industrials. They have SOX obligations and an internal audit team that reports to the board.

**They started in spreadsheets.** Lennar says it plainly: before AuditBoard, the team ran audit in Excel. Estée Lauder replaced older audit software that held the team back.

**Audit, risk and compliance sit together.** Humana and Teradata talk about connecting risk across teams, not only running audits.
