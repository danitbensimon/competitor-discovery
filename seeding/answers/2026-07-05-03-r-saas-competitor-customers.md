their customer list is mostly public if you know where to look:

- case studies + customers page on their site (their proudest accounts)
- g2/capterra reviews — reviewers show company size, industry, often the employer
- job postings requiring experience with their product = current customers
- linkedin: search their product name in skills / experience sections
- builtwith etc. if the product touches the website

worth doing properly: we ran outbound at companies we'd confirmed were using marketo (found via case studies + job posts) with switch-focused messaging, and it converted cold-to-opportunity about 10x better than generic ICP outbound. knowing what they already use changes the whole conversation.

if you don't want to do it manually, i built competitorcustomer.com that does exactly this — give it a competitor and it finds their customers with sources.
