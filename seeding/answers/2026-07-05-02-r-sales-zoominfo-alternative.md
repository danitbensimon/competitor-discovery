before paying for another database, ask what you actually need the list for. if it's "companies using X" or "companies likely to switch off a competitor", you can build it free:

- job postings: search the tool name on linkedin jobs / indeed. "3+ years with outreach" = current customer
- vendor case study pages: their best customers, published by them
- g2: filter a competitor's reviewers by company size. confirmed buyers, known pain (read the 2-star reviews)

apollo is the usual zoominfo replacement and fine for contact data + emails. cognism if you need EU mobile numbers. but every database decays the day you export from it — the signal sources above don't. i'd rather work 300 accounts i know use a competitor than 10k "icp matches" from any database.
