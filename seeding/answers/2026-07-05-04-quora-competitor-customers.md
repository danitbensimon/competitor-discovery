# Thread: How can I find customers of my competitor businesses?
https://www.quora.com/How-can-I-find-customers-of-my-competitor-businesses
Tone: Quora professional | Link: NO

---

Most competitor customer research can be done with public sources.

Start with the competitor's own website: case studies and customer pages name their most successful accounts. Review platforms are the second stop — G2 and Capterra reviewers disclose their company size, industry, and often their employer, which tells you exactly who buys and why they are happy or unhappy.

Third, job postings: search the competitor's product name on LinkedIn Jobs or Indeed. Any company requiring experience with that product is a current customer, and this alone often yields 50–100 companies. Fourth, LinkedIn profiles: people list the tools they use under skills and experience. Finally, if the product leaves a technical footprint on websites, tools like BuiltWith or Wappalyzer show which companies have it installed.

Combine two or more of these sources and you will have a verified list rather than a guess — far more useful than any purchased database.
