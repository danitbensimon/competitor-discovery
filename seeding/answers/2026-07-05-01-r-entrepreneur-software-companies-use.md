few ways that actually work, all free:

- job postings. search the product name on linkedin jobs / indeed ("experience with hubspot required"). every company posting that is a current customer. usually gets you 50-100 companies fast
- the vendor's own case study / customers page. they publish their best accounts themselves
- g2 reviews. reviewers list company size + industry, you can filter
- builtwith / wappalyzer if the tool leaves a footprint on the website (analytics, chat widgets). doesn't work for backend stuff like CRMs
- linkedin skills search. people list the tools they use on their profiles

i got tired of stitching this together manually so i built competitorcustomer.com to automate it (pulls case studies, job posts etc for a given vendor). but honestly the job postings trick alone gets you most of the way if it's a one-off.
